LivingContext is a knowledge layer for Salesforce teams and their AI tools. It brings together synced metadata, an interactive dependency graph and your team's documentation, so you can investigate a change and preserve what you learn.

Use **Graph** to explore where a field, report or email template is referenced and what it depends on. Use the **documentation agent** to draft explanations from linked metadata, review proposed edits and refresh documentation when its sources change. Available connections, permissions and workspace settings determine the context each workflow can use.

## How it works

1. **Connect your tools.** Link GitHub or GitLab (where your docs live) and optionally Salesforce, Confluence, and Slack.
2. **Agents observe.** Every 15 minutes, LivingContext sweeps tracked branches and Salesforce metadata for changes and documentation gaps.
3. **Review in the Inbox.** Proposed updates and new pages land in a triage queue. Accept or reject each one — accepted work ships as a pull request or change request.
4. **Investigate and maintain context.** Explore metadata relationships in Graph, review suggested documentation changes, and browse or ask questions against the context your workspace has indexed.

## The review principle

Every AI-generated change in LivingContext is **grounded or it doesn't ship**: drafts are grounded in real evidence (commit diffs, metadata, your existing docs) and always pass through human review — the [Inbox](/guides/inbox), a [change request](/guides/editing-and-change-requests), or a pull request — before reaching your documentation.
